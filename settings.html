About Us

About BBNL

BBNL is a community-driven consortium of tech-savvy cable operators who came together in Bangalore to deliver unbiased, high-quality internet services, supported by strong local expertise. Our journey began in 1987 with cable television, growing alongside India's satellite TV revolution. As technology evolved, our focus shifted toward delivering better services for households, leading to the rollout of unlimited broadband internet in Bangalore in 2009.

Our History

In 2010, BBNL was formally established to provide Digital Cable TV and Broadband Internet under one trusted network. With decades of hands-on experience, quality of service has always remained our core principle earning BBNL a reputation as a reliable household name.

Our Services

We provide Digital Cable TV and Broadband Internet under one trusted network. BBNL brings together a nationwide fiber network and local internet providers to give you faster, more reliable connectivity at home. Our services include Unlimited Data, 400+ Live TV Channels, HD Channels, and OTTs.
